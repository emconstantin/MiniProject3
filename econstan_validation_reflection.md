# Validation Reflection

For the validation step, I compared Devin's labels with the project histories and recorded the evidence separately from my interpretation. I found that a gap in commits does not automatically mean a project died or recovered. For example, a one-month gap in `ohdsi_patientlevelprediction` or `seung-lab_cloud-volume` is different from the 23-month gap in `google-research_torchsde`. The length of the gap, the activity afterward, and the type of changes all matter.

The hardest fields to interpret were why a project recovered and what its recent work focused on. A commit message can describe a specific fix, but that does not necessarily explain why the contributor returned. Similarly, a project can contain bug fixes, documentation, and releases within the same period. I learned to distinguish what the commits directly show from the broader explanation I assign to them.

My validation results are preliminary. I still need to compare the disputed labels with Devin and record the final decisions before claiming that those differences have been resolved.
