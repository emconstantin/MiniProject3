# Agreement Reflection

The agreement statistics show that some fields were much easier to evaluate consistently than others. We agreed on 70% of the activity-pattern labels, but only 60% of the recovery and current-status labels. Recent themes had 40% agreement, and our explanations for why projects recovered did not match exactly. This suggests that interpreting the meaning of activity is harder than identifying activity itself.

The differences also show the limits of simple agreement percentages. For example, Cohen's kappa was negative for `HasRecovered` even though we agreed on six of ten projects. The category distributions matter, not just the number of matching answers. For `WhyRecovered`, the author values were detailed explanations while the validator values were broad categories, so an exact text comparison does not provide a useful measure of conceptual agreement.

These are pre-discussion results. The post-discussion statistics are still unavailable because no adjudicated outcomes have been recorded. I cannot describe the disagreements as resolved until the final labels are entered.
